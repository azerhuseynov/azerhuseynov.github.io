Dashboards

A few data visualization and analysis dashboards, built in Tableau.

Hotel Booking Cancellations
Exploratory dashboard analyzing ~119K hotel bookings, covering cancellation patterns by country, customer type, and market segment, to flag high-risk segments and estimate revenue lost to cancellations.
View on Tableau Public
Tourist Satisfaction Survey Analysis
Analysis of tourist satisfaction survey responses, exploring rating patterns by demographic segment and survey validity, with a geographic breakdown of respondent locations.
View on Tableau Public
Retail Sales Performance Dashboard
Sales, profit, and shipping performance analysis for a retail dataset, breaking down orders by date, shipping mode, and region.
